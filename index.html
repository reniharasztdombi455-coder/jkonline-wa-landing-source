Clear Information.
Informed Decisions.

Every opportunity should come with a clear explanation of its responsibilities and conditions. Contact the team to request the current role description and verify the details before proceeding.

  • Understand the actual tasks and expected outcomes.
  • Confirm schedule, eligibility and any experience requirements.
  • Ask how work is reviewed and how compensation is determined, if applicable.
  • Never pay an application fee or share passwords or verification codes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get a clearer picture before you contact the team.

Are these positions remote?

Work arrangements vary by opportunity. Contact the team to confirm whether a specific role is remote and what equipment or location requirements apply.

How do I learn about compensation?

Ask for the current written terms, including the payment basis, schedule, eligibility conditions and any applicable deductions, before accepting work.

Do I need previous experience?

Requirements differ by role. Request the current description to confirm whether training or prior experience is required.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Include the position code shown on this page and ask for the complete role description. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive financial information.
